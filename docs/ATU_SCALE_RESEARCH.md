## Demo seed vs real scale

Current Meridiqa ATU demo remains a **slice** (order of tens of courses / lecturers / students) shaped like ATU’s 5-faculty / multi-department structure. Stage 5 performance work should assume ~30k–36k students and ~16 departments when adding summary tables and job batching — not invent larger seed dumps that would break local demos.
